Building a team to advance computational materials chemistry

Prospective Students & Postdocs

I am looking for motivated researchers with a background in computational chemistry, physics, or materials science who are excited about using and developing atomistic simulation methods to address real materials challenges. Prior experience with molecular dynamics, DFT, or machine learning is valued but not required — curiosity and rigour matter more.

A core focus of the group will be machine learning interatomic potentials for ionic and electrochemical systems, alongside ab initio molecular dynamics of solid-state electrolytes and electrode–electrolyte interfaces. If you are drawn to questions at the intersection of statistical mechanics, quantum chemistry, and energy materials, I would love to hear from you.

Positions will open once a faculty or research scientist appointment is confirmed. Prospective PhD students and postdocs are warmly encouraged to reach out early — including to discuss fellowship opportunities such as NSF GRFP, DOE SCGSR, or MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships.
