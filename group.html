Prospective Researchers

As I prepare for independent faculty positions, our future research group will focus on the convergence of statistical physics, machine learning potentials, and high-performance computing to design next-generation energy storage materials.

We are actively looking forward to working with passionate, curious researchers across all career stages:

  • Postdoctoral Researchers: Candidates with a strong background in electronic structure theory (DFT), neural network interatomic potentials (DeepMD, NequIP, Allegro), or rare-event enhanced sampling simulations are encouraged to reach out to discuss potential fellowship applications.
  • Graduate Students: Students interested in computational physical chemistry, molecular dynamics, and physics-informed AI. Strong analytical thinking and programming enthusiasm (Python, C++, Linux) are valued.
  • Undergraduate Researchers: Motivated students seeking hands-on research experience in molecular modeling, data analysis, and high-performance computing.

To inquire about future opportunities or explore potential joint fellowship proposals, please send an email with your CV and a brief summary of your research interests to harender.dhattarwal@rutgers.edu.
