Prospective Opportunities

Future Research Group Vision

As I prepare for independent faculty positions, our future group will focus on the convergence of theoretical chemistry, physics-informed machine learning, and high-performance supercomputing. We aim to build predictive, multi-scale computational frameworks to design next-generation solid electrolytes, electrified interfaces, and sustainable functional fluids.

Prospective Postdocs

Researchers with expertise in equivariant neural networks, electronic structure (DFT), or enhanced sampling molecular dynamics seeking collaborative postdoctoral opportunities are encouraged to reach out.

Graduate Students

Students passionate about statistical mechanics, Python/C++ scientific development, and machine learning applications in chemistry and materials science.

Undergraduate Researchers

Motivated undergraduates in chemistry, physics, chemical engineering, or computer science interested in learning molecular simulations and machine learning.
