Academic Overview

I am a postdoctoral research associate in the group of Prof. Richard C. Remsing at the Department of Chemistry and Chemical Biology, Rutgers University. My research bridges statistical mechanics, quantum chemistry, and machine learning to understand molecular and ionic phenomena in complex condensed-matter systems.

My current efforts focus on developing physics-aware machine learning interatomic potentials that explicitly incorporate long-range polarization, unraveling fast-ion transport mechanisms (such as electronic paddlewheels) in solid-state electrolytes, and designing electrified electrochemical interfaces for safe, high-energy-density batteries and supercapacitors.

I completed my Ph.D. in Chemistry at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Delhi in 2022 under the mentorship of Prof. Hemant K. Kashyap, where I investigated the structure, dynamics, and thermodynamics of ionic liquids, deep eutectic solvents, and superconcentrated electrolytes using molecular dynamics and ab initio simulations. During my doctoral studies, I also conducted collaborative research with Prof. Jer-Lai Kuo at the Institute of Atomic and Molecular Sciences (IAMS), Academia Sinica, Taiwan. Prior to IIT Delhi, I earned an M.Sc. in Chemistry from IIT Guwahati (2017) and a B.Sc. (Honours) in Chemistry from Ramjas College, University of Delhi (2015).
