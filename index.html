Focus Areas

Research Themes

Machine Learning Interatomic Potentials

Developing neural network potentials with accurate long-range electrostatics, enabling ab initio-quality simulations of dielectric saturation and polar liquids at molecular dynamics scale.

Learn more

Solid-State Electrolytes & Superionic Conductors

Uncovering the role of electronic paddle-wheel mechanisms and hidden quantum disorder in driving superionic conduction — with implications for next-generation solid-state batteries.

Learn more

Electrochemical Interfaces & Electrolyte Design

Resolving atomic-scale structure at electrode-electrolyte interfaces in ionic liquids, water-in-salt electrolytes, and superconcentrated systems to advance energy storage and carbon capture.
