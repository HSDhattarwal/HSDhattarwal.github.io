Research Focus

Core Research Pillars

Bridging quantum mechanics, statistical physics, and machine learning to pioneer next-generation energy materials.

ML Interatomic Potentials & Long-Range Interactions

Developing equivariant neural network potentials that incorporate long-range polarization and electrostatic response. Enabling ab initio-fidelity simulations of water under extreme electric fields and dielectric saturation.

DeepMD Dielectric Saturation Polar Liquids

Solid-State Electrolytes & Superionic Conduction

Unraveling non-classical ion transport mechanisms such as electronic paddle-wheels, cation clustering, and quantum disorder in superionic conductors (AgI, thiophosphates) for safe, high-capacity all-solid-state batteries.

Solid-State Batteries Paddlewheel Conduction AIMD

Electrochemical Interfaces & Confined Systems

Investigating the microscopic structure of water-in-salt electrolytes (WiSE), ionic liquids, and deep eutectic solvents (DES) at electrified carbon/metal electrodes and within nanoscale hydrophobic confinement.
