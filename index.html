Research interests

I study how electrostatics and electronic structure shape the behaviour of materials, from ion motion in solids to molecular organisation at interfaces.

Physically faithful atomistic models

I build long-range machine-learning interatomic potentials that keep electrostatics consistent across heterogeneous environments, with a focus on the interfaces that matter for catalysis and batteries.

Electronic routes to ion transport

I trace how the electron density takes part in ionic conduction, from the electronic paddle-wheel mechanism in superionic silver iodide to polarisation and screening in the melt.
