About Harender

Harender S. Dhattarwal is a postdoctoral associate in the group of Professor Richard Remsing at Rutgers University. Harender received his B.Sc. in chemistry from the Ramjas College, Delhi University in 2015. He then went to the Indian Institute of Techonology Guwahati for his M.Sc. in chemistry in 2017. Harender received his Ph.D. in Chemistry from Indian Institute of Techonology Delhi, India. During that time, he conducted research in the lab of Professor Hemant Kashyap in collaboration with Prof. Jer-Lai Kuo at IAMS, Taiwan. His research was focused on understanding the structure and stability of modern electrolytes in confinements and near interfaces.

Harender's current work in Rick's group focuses on long-range machine learning potentials for molecular dynamics simulations. He is also exploring the transport mechanism of superionic materials.
